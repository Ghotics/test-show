Process

How a partnership takes shape

Four stages, no shortcuts. The line fills as you scroll.

1

Originate

Our best deals come from relationships years in the making. We'd rather know a company long before it's for sale.

2

Diligence

We go deep on unit economics, people and durability — and we're honest early when the fit isn't there.

3

Partner

Aligned terms, a clear plan, and a small set of priorities we pursue shoulder-to-shoulder with management.

4

Compound

We stay patient, reinvest behind what's working, and let time and discipline do the heavy lifting.
